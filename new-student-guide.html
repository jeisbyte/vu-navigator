New Student Campus Guide

Welcome to Victoria University! Use this guide to quickly orient yourself around Market Plaza Tower, locate your lecture rooms, and find key university offices on your first day.

Know Your Starting Point

The primary campus entrance and security desk are on the Ground Floor. Start here when planning your route.

Identify Your Floor

Rooms are grouped by floor level (e.g. 100s are on Floor 1, 200s on Floor 2, 300s on Floor 3, up to Floor 9).

03

Search for Any Room

Use the search tool on the home page or directions directory to type any room number, lecturer office, or lab.

Step-by-Step Directions

First-Day Orientation Tips

  • Note your lecture room code and floor before heading to campus each morning.
  • Elevators are located near the main entrance; stairs are accessible on each floor for quick transit.
  • If you are unsure of your lecture room, visit the Student Support Office on Ground Floor (G02).
  • For student ID cards, tuition payment clearances, or timetable questions, visit Academic Registration in Basement 1 (B105).
