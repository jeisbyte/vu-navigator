ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Campus Navigation Made Simple

Victoria University Tower Navigator is an official digital initiative designed to help students, faculty, administrative staff, and visitors navigate the multi-level Market Plaza campus tower with confidence.

THE CHALLENGE

Navigating a Multi-Level Urban Campus

Large multi-story university towers can be challenging to navigate, especially for new students, guest lecturers, and visitors attending campus events. VU Navigator provides direct access to SVG architectural layouts, room rosters, and precise walking directions from the ground entrance.

THE SOLUTION

Search. Identify. Arrive.

Featuring live room search, floor-by-floor maps (Basement 2 through Floor 9), room photo previews, and a dedicated new student orientation guide, the navigator ensures seamless movement across every academic department.
